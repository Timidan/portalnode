PortalNode
Claims Portaldot staking rewards before they expire, and tells operators when their node needs attention.
01It sends the payouts
Real payoutStakersByPage transactions from a fee-only key, every era, settled only from finalized receipts.
02Anyone can claim
Look up any validator or nominator on V3 or mainnet; one click opens the payout batch in Node Console for your own wallet.
03Health on-chain
Each era a Solidity contract on V3 records every validator's paid-through era and liveness for any dApp to read.
Plus: liveness as pallet-im-online counts it, session-key checks, Telegram/Discord alerts with the fix, a setup wizard

Why Portaldot is essential

Built on what Portaldot runs, not around it.

staking pallet
payoutStakersByPage on V3 and payoutStakers on mainnet runtime 1002, chosen from the runtime's shape
pallet-revive (V3 EVM)
the ValidatorHealth contract, written each era by the keeper's own sr25519 key through revive.call
pallet-im-online + session
liveness exactly as the chain counts it, warnings before a validator is reported offline, key checks
Node Console
every user-signed action (claims, the setup batch) opens in Portaldot's own console; no custom wallet
The gap exists because of how Portaldot pays: rewards are pulled by a payout transaction and expire after 84 eras. PortalNode closes that gap for every validator and nominator, on both networks.

Who it serves
